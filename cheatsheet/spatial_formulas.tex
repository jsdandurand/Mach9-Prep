\sect{Vectors}
\[
\|v\|=\sqrt{v\cdot v},\quad
\hat v=\frac{v}{\|v\|},\quad
\operatorname{proj}_{b}a=\frac{a\cdot b}{b\cdot b}\,b
\]
\[
\cos\theta=\mathrm{clip}\!\left(\frac{a\cdot b}{\|a\|\|b\|},-1,1\right)
\]
\[
\text{area}_{\triangle}=\tfrac12\|a\times b\|
\]
\[
a\times b=
\begin{vmatrix}
\mathbf i&\mathbf j&\mathbf k\\
a_x&a_y&a_z\\
b_x&b_y&b_z
\end{vmatrix}
\quad
[a,b,c]=a\cdot(b\times c)
\]
2D side of directed $ab$: $(b-a)\times(p-a)=(b_x-a_x)(p_y-a_y)-(b_y-a_y)(p_x-a_x)$.
Positive is left. Right-hand offset is the negation.
Zero-length $v$: angle and $\hat v$ are undefined.

\sect{Rotations and rigid motions}
Right-hand rule, radians. Unit axis $k$, skew $K$ with $Kv=k\times v$:
\[
K=\begin{pmatrix}0&-k_z&k_y\\ k_z&0&-k_x\\ -k_y&k_x&0\end{pmatrix}
\]
\begin{align*}
R&=I+\sin\theta\,K+(1-\cos\theta)\,K^{2}\\
&=\cos\theta\,I+\sin\theta\,K+(1-\cos\theta)\,kk^{\mathsf T}
\end{align*}
$K^{2}=kk^{\mathsf T}-I$ for $\|k\|=1$. Do not mix the two forms.
\[
R_z(\theta)=\begin{pmatrix}c&-s&0\\ s&c&0\\ 0&0&1\end{pmatrix}
\quad
c=\cos\theta,\; s=\sin\theta
\]
$+90^{\circ}$ about $+Z$ sends $(1,0,0)\to(0,1,0)$.
$+120^{\circ}$ about $(1,1,1)$ cycles $x\to y\to z$.

Valid rotation: $R^{\mathsf T}R=I$ and $\det R=+1$. $\det=-1$ is a reflection.

Apply first, then second, on column vectors:
\[
R=R_2R_1,\qquad t=R_2 t_1+t_2
\]
Inverse of $p'=Rp+t$:
\[
R^{-1}=R^{\mathsf T},\qquad t_{\mathrm{inv}}=-R^{\mathsf T}t
\]
Quaternion $(\,w,x,y,z\,)=(\cos(\theta/2),\,\sin(\theta/2)\,k)$, then convert to $R$. Stay with matrices unless asked.

\sect{Section frame (Z up)}
Horizontal unit tangent $(t_x,t_y)$. Columns of $R$ are forward, left, up:
\[
f=(t_x,t_y,0),\quad
\ell=(-t_y,t_x,0),\quad
u=(0,0,1)
\]
\[
f\times\ell=u
\]
Right normal, positive offset: $r=(t_y,-t_x)$. Eastbound road: south is right. $\det R=+1$.

\sect{Point, line, segment, plane}
\sub{Plane} through $p_0$ with unit $n$: $n\cdot(x-p_0)=0$.
\[
d_{\mathrm{signed}}=(p-p_0)\cdot n
\qquad
d=|d_{\mathrm{signed}}|
\]
Three points: $n=(b-a)\times(c-a)$, then normalize. Angle between planes $=$ angle between normals.

\sub{Line} through $a$ toward $b$:
\begin{align*}
t&=\frac{(p-a)\cdot(b-a)}{\|b-a\|^{2}}\\
c&=a+t(b-a),\qquad d=\|p-c\|
\end{align*}
\sub{Segment:} clip $t$ to $[0,1]$. Unclipped $t$ is the infinite line. If $a\approx b$, distance is $\|p-a\|$.

\sub{Skew lines} $p_1+s\,d_1$ and $p_2+t\,d_2$:
\[
d=\frac{|(p_2-p_1)\cdot(d_1\times d_2)|}{\|d_1\times d_2\|}
\]

\sub{Ray} $o+td$, plane $(p_0,n)$:
\[
t=\frac{(p_0-o)\cdot n}{d\cdot n}
\]
Parallel if $|d\cdot n|\approx 0$. In front of the origin: $t>0$.

\sect{Fits}
\sub{Plane, SVD.} Center $C=P-\bar p$. Normal is the last right singular vector of $C$ (least variance), $V_t[-1]$. Flip so the largest component is positive. General plane $ax+by+cz=d$. The graph $z=ax+by+c$ cannot represent a vertical curb face. One outlier tilts a least-squares fit.

\sub{Line, PCA.} Direction $=$ top eigenvector of the covariance. A point on the line is the centroid.

\sub{RANSAC.} Sample 3 points, skip a near-zero cross product, keep the largest inlier count, refit SVD, recompute the mask.

\sub{Circle (K\r{a}sa),} plan view:
\[
x^{2}+y^{2}=2 c_x x+2 c_y y+c,\qquad
r^{2}=c+c_x^{2}+c_y^{2}
\]
Use \texttt{lstsq}, not $(A^{\mathsf T}A)^{-1}$. A perfect arc recovers $(c,r)$. The centroid of a one-sided arc sits on the visible side, and $\sim$1\,cm of noise on a short arc moves the fit.

\sect{Rays and triangles}
M\"oller--Trumbore. $e_1=v_1-v_0$, $e_2=v_2-v_0$, direction need not be a unit vector:
\begin{align*}
h&=d\times e_2 & a&=e_1\cdot h & f&=1/a\\
s&=o-v_0 & u&=f(s\cdot h) & q&=s\times e_1\\
v&=f(d\cdot q) & t&=f(e_2\cdot q)
\end{align*}
Hit when $t>\eps$, $u\ge 0$, $v\ge 0$, $u+v\le 1$. $|a|\approx 0$: parallel. $t$ is the scale in $o+td$.

\sect{Boxes}
AABB, per axis: $\ell=\max(0,\;\min(a_{\max},b_{\max})-\max(a_{\min},b_{\min}))$.
\[
\mathrm{IoU}=\frac{\prod \ell_i}{\mathrm{vol}\,A+\mathrm{vol}\,B-\prod \ell_i}
\]
A shared face has volume 0.

OBB: columns of $R$ are the box axes in world coordinates. Local of a row point is $(p-c)\,R$, i.e.\ $R^{\mathsf T}(p-c)$. Inside when every $|local_i|\le h_i$.

\sect{Barycentric $Z$ (TIN)}
Query $p$ against triangle $a,b,c$ in XY. $v_0=b-a$, $v_1=c-a$, $v_2=p-a$, $d_{ij}=v_i\cdot v_j$.
\begin{align*}
D&=d_{00}d_{11}-d_{01}^{2}\\
v&=(d_{11}d_{20}-d_{01}d_{21})/D\\
w&=(d_{00}d_{21}-d_{01}d_{20})/D\\
u&=1-v-w
\end{align*}
Inside, boundary included, when $u,v,w\ge 0$ and $D\not\approx 0$. Then $z=u z_a+v z_b+w z_c$. First hit wins; a shared edge of a continuous mesh agrees. A breakline constrains the mesh, not the query. Drop a face with any edge over the length cap.

\sect{Kabsch and one ICP step}
$P$ and $Q$ correspond, same order. $H=P_c^{\mathsf T}Q_c$ is $3\times 3$.
\[
H=U\Sigma V^{\mathsf T},\quad
R=VU^{\mathsf T}
\]
If $\det R<0$, flip the last row of $V^{\mathsf T}$ (smallest singular vector) and rebuild $R$.
\[
t=\bar q-R\bar p
\qquad
Q\approx (RP^{\mathsf T})^{\mathsf T}+t
\]
One ICP step: nearest neighbor source$\to$target, then Kabsch. True matches only if the motion is small relative to point spacing.

\sect{Camera ($+Z$ forward)}
$p_c=Rp_w+t$. Intrinsics $K$. Pixel $(u,v)$ from $q=Kp_c$, then $u,v=q_{0,1}/q_2$.
Keep $z>0$ and $0\le u<W$, $0\le v<H$. Image shape is $(H,W)$; $u$ is the column.
Back-project depth stored as camera $z$:
\[
\begin{pmatrix}x\\y\\z\end{pmatrix}
=z\,K^{-1}\begin{pmatrix}u\\v\\1\end{pmatrix}
\]
Do not divide by $z\le 0$.

\sect{Polylines, station, section}
Station is horizontal XY chainage. $Z$ does not advance it. Clipped $t$ on segment $i$ gives station $s_i+tL_i\in[0,L]$.
\[
\mathrm{offset}=(p-c)\cdot(t_y,-t_x)
\]
Positive is right of increasing station. Ties take the lowest segment index. Past an end, station clamps; offset is the lateral part of $(p-\mathrm{endpoint})$, not $\|p-c\|$.

Vertical section at an alignment origin:
\[
\mathrm{along}=(\mathrm{xy}-o)\cdot\hat t,\qquad
\mathrm{offset}=(\mathrm{xy}-o)\cdot r
\]
Keep $|\mathrm{along}|\le$ thickness and $|\mathrm{offset}|\le$ half-width. Elevation is absolute $Z$. Interior vertex: outgoing tangent. Final vertex: incoming tangent.

Even--odd polygon test, ray $+x$: count an edge when exactly one end is strictly above the point and the crossing is to the right. Skip $dy=0$. The boundary is unstable.
Douglas--Peucker: keep endpoints; keep an interior vertex whose distance to the span exceeds $\eps$.
Segments properly cross when each pair of endpoints is strictly on opposite sides of the other line. A shared vertex is not a crossing.

\sect{Clouds and local shape}
Neighbors: $\|A_{:,\mathrm{none},:}-B_{\mathrm{none},:,:}\|$ is $(N,M)$. $N=M=10^{4}$ is $\sim$800\,MB. Chunk, or use a KD-tree.
Chamfer here: $\mathrm{mean}_A\min_B+\mathrm{mean}_B\min_A$.

Voxel key $\lfloor p/s\rfloor$. $\lfloor-0.1\rfloor=-1$, but a cast toward zero gives $0$. Centroid $=$ bin sum $/$ count. \texttt{np.add.at} accumulates repeats; \texttt{sums[idx] +=} does not. Min-$Z$ grid: same half-open bins, row 0 at minimum $Y$, \texttt{np.minimum.at}.

Covariance $(X^{\mathsf T}X)/N$. \texttt{eigh} returns $\lambda$ ascending; reorder $\lambda_1\ge\lambda_2\ge\lambda_3$.
\[
L=\frac{\lambda_1-\lambda_2}{\lambda_1},\quad
P=\frac{\lambda_2-\lambda_3}{\lambda_1},\quad
S=\frac{\lambda_3}{\lambda_1}
\]
Direction $=$ largest eigenvector, normal $=$ smallest. Signs are arbitrary. Pole: high $L$, $|\mathrm{dir}\cdot up|\approx 1$. Ground: high $P$, $|n\cdot up|\approx 1$. Curb face: high $P$, $|n\cdot up|\approx 0$. If $\lambda_1\approx 0$, return zeros.

\sect{Before you write}
State $(N,3)$ or $(3,N)$. Clip $\cos$ before $\arccos$. Guard every denominator. $t<0$ is behind the ray origin. $\det R=-1$ is a reflection. SVD and eigenvector signs flip. A centimetre tolerance is not $10^{-8}$.
